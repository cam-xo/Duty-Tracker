What this tool does & doesn't do
  • Calculations follow the FDP, cumulative duty/flight time, LNO and standby limits set out in your FMS manual (based on CAO 48.1 Appendix 4B).
  • Max FDP is looked up from Table 6 by start time, then adjusted for split duty rest, extensions and prior NFD, capped at 16 hrs.
  • Split duty rest is calculated automatically: a gap of at least 2 consecutive hours immediately after a prior FDP (not NFD) within the same standby period qualifies, extending the later FDP's limit. It is not applied to an increased FDP, per 10.6.1.
  • Conservative assumption: within that rest gap, only the portion falling between 2100–0600 is treated as genuine sleeping accommodation (full credit, per Table 7) — it's unrealistic to assume someone actually sleeps during the day even though sleeping accommodation is available. The portion falling 0600–2100 is treated as resting accommodation only (half credit, capped at 2 hours total, per Table 8). The full raw gap (unweighted) is still what counts at 50% toward cumulative duty per Table 12.
  • The 40-vs-60-hour 7-day duty cap checks for a 36+ hour off-duty gap that fully contains at least 2 local nights (an 8+ hour break covering 2200–0500), based on your rosters being in a single time zone.
  • Late Night Operations are counted once per local night regardless of how many FDPs occurred, per the note under 10.13.
  • Cumulative duty windows clip entries to the window boundary and merge overlapping entries, so a duty straddling the 7- or 14-day cutoff counts only for the portion inside it.
  • The Time Line's "required rest" band only follows a rostered Standby period, anchored at the later of the standby's own end or the end of any NFD/FDP that started during it (Table 10 base by completion time, plus extra for an FDP running over 12 hrs, using an extension, or an increased FDP's 12-hour floor). Shortfalls show as a red hatched "rest conflict" band. Standalone FDPs with no enclosing standby don't get a rest band on the Time Line.
  • The Off-Duty Period tile in Live Status checks rest after the most recent FDP or standby as of the day being viewed (not just standby periods, unlike the Time Line's per-standby view) — the two are worth reading together rather than expecting them to always agree. It only ever looks at what's most recent for the selected day; it does not surface older unresolved shortfalls from further back in your history. The 14-day/21-day requirements in Tables 9 and 11 are still not checked anywhere.
  • The Duty tile's maximum is a local convention, not a rule from the manual: Table 6 is defined against FDP start time, and this applies it to the standby start time instead. Treat it as a rough reference only.
  • A KSS score of 7 recorded at either the start or end of an FDP adds a mandatory 3-hour rest (10.5) shown above the line on the Time Line, immediately after that task. Any NFD or FDP starting during that window is flagged as a conflict, same as the other rest-conflict bands.
  • Split duty's "as if commenced by a new FCM" constraint (10.8.1) is not checked, and the reduction of a calculated off-duty period to 12 hours (10.12.3) is not modelled.
  • The orange marker line on today's Time Line shows the max FDP end time for the current, open, or a hypothetical now-started FDP. Yellow extension-limit lines only appear when an actual open FDP has an extension type selected on it — they're not shown as a generic "what's available" hint.
  • An FDP logged with "Task still in progress" ticked has no end time yet — its duration, timeline band, and all totals stay live and keep growing until you edit it to add the actual end time. The "Must land by" banner and the marker lines above both use this open entry as their reference the moment one exists.
  • If an FDP starts less than 2 hours after the previous FDP in the same standby ends (including a zero or overlapping gap), it's treated as a continuation of the same unbroken duty period rather than a fresh FDP — its Table 6 lookup, day-shift 2200 cap, and "Must land by" deadline all anchor to when that continuous duty period actually began, not to this individual entry's own start time. The Duty tile merges overlapping tasks the same way, so it stays consistent with LIVE FDP.
  • An open task left un-closed for more than 14 hours triggers a red warning on the banner and an "OPEN Xh ⚠" badge in the Duty Log, since an open FDP's duration keeps growing forever until closed and can otherwise silently distort every cumulative total. Having more than one open FDP at once also triggers its own warning listing them, since that's normally a data-entry slip.
  • Saving checks for entries added by another device since this one last loaded and preserves them, rather than blindly overwriting — protects against losing data if you're using this on two devices close together. It can't resolve two devices editing the exact same entry at the same moment (last save wins for that entry) — full conflict resolution would need real-time listeners.
  • Import Backup now also catches duplicates by matching type/date/start/end time, not just by ID — so re-importing the same duty under a freshly generated ID (e.g. from a different export) won't create a second copy.
  • Per 10.6, an open FDP within a Day Shift standby (detected from the standby's notes containing "Day Shift") has its max capped at 2200 — the "Must land by" banner and orange marker line reflect this. Select the Day Shift past 22:00 extension option once Duty Officer/Chief Pilot approval is given to lift the cap back to the normal calculated limit, shown as a yellow marker line.
  • This is a personal support tool. Air Maestro remains your company's official record per the FMS manual — always log there too.
  • Data is stored privately to your account only, in this artifact's storage — not shared with anyone else.
  • Use Export Backup periodically to save your entries to a file, and Import Backup to restore them — this protects you if data ever fails to carry over when this tool is updated.
